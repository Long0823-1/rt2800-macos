Installation complete

A Wi-Fi icon appears in the menu bar. Click it and choose a network. Networks you connect to are remembered and joined automatically next time.

If the icon does not appear, open “RT2800 Wi-Fi” from the Applications folder.

Your normal connection stays as it is. To send all traffic through this adapter, choose “Use as Default Route” in the menu.

Uninstall: sudo /usr/local/libexec/rt2800/uninstall.sh
